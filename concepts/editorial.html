Our book for October

Padma Nadir Majhi

Manik Bandopadhyay, known in English as The Boatman of the Padma

Kuber fishes the Padma at night for hilsa he will never be able to afford. Around him, the village of Ketupur lives on the river’s mood: floods, debts, marriages and the quiet trader Hossain Miah, who is building something on an island called Moynadwip.

We picked it because the rains have stretched into October and the river is on everyone’s mind. It is short, unsentimental and still the truest thing written about the people who feed this city.

Read it in one or two sittings while it is still raining. It was written to be heard over the sound of water.

Rafiq Hasan, behind the counter since 1998
